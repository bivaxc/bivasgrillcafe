About Biva's

A place for good food and good company.

Biva's Grill & Cafe is a modern local grill and cafe in Kagadi, built around freshly prepared food, chilled drinks and a welcoming atmosphere.

Whether you are grabbing a quick Rolex, meeting friends, watching football or sitting down for a conversation, Biva's is designed to be more than just a place to eat.
